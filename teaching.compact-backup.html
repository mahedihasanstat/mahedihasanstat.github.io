Teaching

I have taught and assisted undergraduate courses in statistics and mathematics at Washington State University, including introductory statistics, statistical methods for research, multivariate statistics, discrete mathematics, and calculus. My teaching emphasizes clear statistical reasoning, reproducible problem solving, and connecting mathematical ideas to real data.
