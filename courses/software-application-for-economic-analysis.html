ECON-4703: Software Application for Economic Analysis

Spring 2018 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Undergraduate

Course Description

This course focused on software-based economic data analysis, including data handling, statistical summaries, visualization, regression, and empirical interpretation for economic applications.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
