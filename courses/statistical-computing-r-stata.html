Statistical Computing (R & STATA)

Fall 2017 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Graduate / Undergraduate

Course Description

This course introduced statistical computing workflows using R and STATA, including data management, visualization, statistical analysis, and reproducible reporting.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
