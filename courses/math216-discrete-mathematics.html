Math 216: Discrete Mathematics

Spring 2022 | Washington State University | Undergraduate

Course Description

This course introduced logic, proof techniques, combinatorics, graph theory, and discrete structures used in mathematics, computer science, and statistical reasoning.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
