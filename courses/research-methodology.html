BUS/ALD-3610: Business Research Methodology

Fall 2016, 2017, 2018 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Graduate / Undergraduate

Course Description

This course covered research design, formulation of research questions, literature review, data collection strategies, analysis planning, and presentation of research findings.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
