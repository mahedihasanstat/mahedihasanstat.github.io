Introduction to Statistics

Spring 2016, 2017, 2018 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Undergraduate

Course Description

This course introduced descriptive statistics, probability concepts, sampling distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, correlation, regression, and applied statistical reasoning.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
