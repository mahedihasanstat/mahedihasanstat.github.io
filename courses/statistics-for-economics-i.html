ECON-104: Statistics for Economics I

Fall 2015 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Undergraduate

Course Description

This course introduced foundational statistical concepts for economics, including data summaries, probability, distributions, estimation, hypothesis testing, and applied interpretation.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
