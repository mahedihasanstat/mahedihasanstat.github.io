Econometrics

Spring 2018, 2019; Fall 2016 | Bangladesh University of Professionals | Graduate / Undergraduate

Course Description

This course introduced econometric modeling, regression analysis, model diagnostics, hypothesis testing, and applications to economic and social science data.

This course was maintained through an online teaching platform such as Canvas, Blackboard, or a similar system, and the course materials are not made available here.
